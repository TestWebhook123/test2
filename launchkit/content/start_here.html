2. Store plan & pricing

Launch 7 products. Five focused planners make you findable for specific searches ("nurse planner", "ADHD planner"), the bundle raises your average order, and the free sampler builds an email list you can sell to all season.

Why these prices

  • $14 per planner. Generic digital planners sell for $5 to $10. Yours are niche, dated, hyperlinked, about 108 pages and come in 4 versions, so they belong in the upper-middle of the market. $14 sits under the "$15 = needs thinking" line and leaves room for a meaningful launch discount.
  • $34 bundle. Five planners bought separately cost $70. $34 is "all five for less than the price of three", easy to say and to see. It's the best option for gift buyers and people who wear two hats (creator + freelancer, nurse + fitness).
  • Free sampler ($0+). Gumroad collects the email of everyone who downloads it. Those people get a follow-up sequence and a 20% code (SAMPLER20). Leave "pay what you want" on with a $3 suggestion; some people tip.
  • Fixed prices, not pay-what-you-want, on the paid planners. A clear price converts better for templates.
  • Don't put prices on images. None of the images in this kit show a price, so you can change prices any time.

3. Account setup (once, about 20 minutes)

  1. Store name and username. Settings → Profile. Pick a calm, planner-friendly name (for example "Plan Well Studio"). Your store lives at username.gumroad.com.
  2. Profile picture. Use a photo of yourself (most trusting) or 2-Gumroad-Images/06 Bundle - All 5 Planners/thumbnail-square.jpg.
  3. Bio (paste): "Planners made for one kind of person, not everyone. Dated {{YEAR}} digital + printable planners for creators, freelancers, fitness, ADHD brains and nurses."
  4. Payouts. Settings → Payments: add your bank or PayPal, and fill in your tax details so payouts aren't held.
  5. Refund policy. Set a 14-day refund window. For a new store with no reviews it builds trust, and very few people ask for refunds on a $14 planner.
  6. Email. Make sure you can reply from the address on your receipts. Buyers will reply to them.

4. Upload a planner (repeat for all 5)

Do the Content Creator Planner first, then repeat for the other four. Every field's text is in 3-Listing-Copy/listing-copy.html; open it in your browser next to Gumroad.

  1. Products → New product → Digital product. Paste the Name and set the Price ($14). Click Next / Customize.
  2. Description: in listing-copy.html press Copy description, click into Gumroad's description box and paste. Headings and bullets come along.
  3. Cover: upload the 5 images from 2-Gumroad-Images/<planner>/ in number order: cover-1-hero first. The first image is what people see in links and search.
  4. Thumbnail: upload thumbnail-square.jpg. It's used on your profile, in Discover and in buyers' libraries.
  5. URL: set the custom URL to the slug in the copy file (for example content-creator-planner-2027).
  6. Summary and Additional details: paste the summary line, then add each detail row (Format, Pages, Dates, Sizes, Week start, Works with).
  7. Content tab: upload the 5 files from 1-Product-Files/<planner>/ (4 planner PDFs + Quick Start Guide). Paste the Content page text above the files.
  8. Share tab → Discover: turn on "list on Discover", pick the category from the copy file (closest match if Gumroad's names differ) and add the 5 tags.
  9. Publish, then test it: create a 100% off code (for example TEST100, limit 1), buy your own product, check that the email and all 5 downloads work, then delete the code.
If Gumroad shows tabs with slightly different names (Gumroad updates its editor now and then), the fields are still the same: name, price, description, cover, thumbnail, URL, summary, details, content files, Discover settings.

5. Bundle & free sampler

Bundle (after the 5 planners are published)

  1. Products → New product → Bundle. Add all 5 planners.
  2. Name, price ($34), description, 3 covers and thumbnail from 06 Bundle - All 5 Planners. No files to upload: buyers automatically get each planner's files.
  3. Publish. If your account has Checkout → Upsells, add an upsell from any single planner to the bundle with the line: "Want all five for $20 more?"

Free sampler

  1. New product → Digital product. Name from the copy file. Price $0 with "pay what you want" allowed (suggested $3).
  2. Upload the sampler PDF from 1-Product-Files/07 Free Sampler, add the 3 covers and thumbnail, paste the description.
  3. In the Content page text, replace each [paste product link] with your real product links.
  4. Publish. Pin it first on your profile; it's the easiest "yes" for new visitors.

Order on your profile

8. Marketing that works for planners

Pinterest (best long-term traffic for planners)

  • Make a free business account. Create one board per niche plus "{{YEAR}} Planners".
  • Pin the images in 5-Marketing-Images/pinterest-pin-1000x1500 and the page previews, using the titles and descriptions in social-captions.txt. Link every pin to the matching product.
  • Pin 1 to 3 times a day. Pins keep bringing traffic for months, and planner searches peak from November to January.

Short video (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)

  • The format that sells planners: a flip-through of the planner on an iPad, a tap on a tab to show the links, filling in a page. Use the hooks and scripts in social-captions.txt.
  • One niche per video. Hook in the first second: "If you're a nurse on rotating shifts, you need this."
  • Use story-reel-cover-1080x1920 images as video covers and stories.

Communities

  • Niche communities (nursing, freelancing, ADHD, fitness, creator groups) love genuinely useful freebies. Share the free sampler, not a sales pitch, and follow each group's self-promotion rules.

Email (built into Gumroad)

  • Workflows: set up the 3-email sequence for sampler downloaders (email-templates.txt).
  • Emails (broadcasts): launch, Black Friday, New Year. The templates are written for you.

9. Your photos & b-roll (optional, but they help)

Everything in this kit works without photos. Real footage makes covers and videos feel more trustworthy. If you share any of these, I can build lifestyle versions of the covers and pins:

  • Horizontal (16:9) desk shots with empty space on one side: desk, coffee, notebook, iPad, plants. Good as cover backgrounds.
  • Vertical (9:16) clips of hands using an iPad with a pencil, turning pages of printed sheets, a printer printing.
  • Niche props: scrubs or a stethoscope (nurse), dumbbells or sneakers (fitness), ring light or camera (creator), laptop with invoices (freelancer), sticky notes or fidget toy (ADHD).
  • Screen recording of you flipping through the planner in GoodNotes or Notability. It's the single most convincing asset.

Numbers to watch (Gumroad Analytics)

  • Views to sales: 2 to 5% is healthy for templates. Below 1% after 500+ views? Test a different first cover or name.
  • Best-selling niche: put more videos and pins into the winner, and consider a second product for that audience (for example a nursing-student planner).
  • Bundle share: if under 15% of orders, make the upsell more visible.
